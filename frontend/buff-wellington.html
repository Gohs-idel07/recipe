Buff Wellington

Serves: 4 | Prep Time: 45 mins | Cook Time: 35 mins

Ingredients

Main Roast & Layering:

  • 1 kg center-cut buff tenderloin (water buffalo meat)
  • 6-8 slices thin ham or bacon strips
  • 500g ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 2 tbsp English mustard or spicy mustard
  • 2 egg yolks, beaten (for egg wash)
  • 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • Salt & crushed black pepper

Mushroom Duxelles:

  • 500g button or wild mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 1 small onion or shallot, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Sear the Buff: Season the buff tenderloin generously with salt and pepper. Heat butter/oil in a pan over high heat. Sear the meat for 45–60 seconds on all sides until nicely browned. Remove from heat and brush with mustard while warm. Let it cool.
  2. Prepare Duxelles: Finely mince the mushrooms and onions. Sauté in a hot pan with garlic and butter over medium-high heat until all excess moisture evaporates into a thick paste. Season with salt and pepper, then allow to cool completely.
  3. Assemble the Roll: Spread cling film on a flat surface. Lay out ham slices overlapping slightly. Spread the cooled mushroom duxelles evenly across the ham layer.
  4. Wrap the Buff: Place the seared buff fillet at one end and roll the ham tightly around it using the cling film. Twist the ends tightly and chill in the fridge for 15 minutes.
  5. Wrap in Puff Pastry: Roll out the puff pastry sheet. Unwrap the buff roll and place it on the pastry. Brush the edges with egg wash, roll tightly, and seal all seams completely. Chill in the fridge for another 15 minutes.
  6. Bake: Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Score the top of the pastry lightly, brush generously with beaten egg yolks, and bake for 25–30 minutes until the pastry is deep golden brown.
  7. Rest & Serve: Allow the Wellington to rest for 10 minutes before slicing into thick slices. Serve hot.
