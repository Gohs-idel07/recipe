Chicken Momo Recipe

Serves: 4 (Makes ~24 Momos) | Prep Time: 40 mins

Ingredients

For the Dough:

  • 2 cups plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup water (as needed)

For the Filling:

  • 400g minced chicken
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 green chillies, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil or melted butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix flour and salt. Gradually add water and knead into a smooth, firm dough. Cover and rest for 20 minutes.
  2. In a separate bowl, combine minced chicken, finely chopped onion, green chillies, ginger-garlic paste, coriander, soy sauce, oil, salt, and pepper. Mix thoroughly.
  3. Divide the dough into small, equal-sized balls. Roll each ball into a thin 3-inch circle, keeping the edges slightly thinner than the centre.
  4. Place 1 tablespoon of filling in the centre of each wrapper. Pleat the edges together and press firmly to seal the momo completely.
  5. Grease a steamer pan with a little oil to prevent sticking. Arrange the momos inside, leaving small gaps between them.
  6. Steam over boiling water for 12–15 minutes until the outer skin turns translucent and non-sticky.
  7. Serve hot with spicy tomato-sesame chutney.
