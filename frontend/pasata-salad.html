Fresh Italian Pasta Salad Recipe

Serves: 4 | Prep Time: 15 mins

Ingredients

Salad Base:

  • 250g rotini or fusilli pasta
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup cucumber, diced
  • 1/2 cup black olives, sliced
  • 1/2 cup feta or mozzarella cheese, cubed
  • 1/4 cup red onion, finely chopped

Zesty Dressing:

  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice or red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water according to package directions until tender. Drain and rinse under cold water to stop cooking.
  2. In a small jar or bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice (or vinegar), minced garlic, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, combine chilled cooked pasta, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, black olives, red onion, and cheese.
  4. Pour the prepared dressing over the pasta mixture.
  5. Toss gently until all ingredients are evenly coated.
  6. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 20 minutes before serving for the best flavor.
