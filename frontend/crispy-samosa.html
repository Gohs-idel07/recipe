Crispy Aloo Samosa Recipe

Serves: 4 (Makes 8 Samosas) | Prep & Fry Time: 50 mins

Ingredients

Dough:

  • 2 cups plain flour (maida)
  • 4 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1/2 tsp carom seeds (ajwain)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Water (to knead stiff dough)

Filling:

  • 3 large potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Oil for deep frying

Cooking Instructions

  1. Rub flour, salt, carom seeds, and oil together with your hands until crumbly. Gradually add cold water and knead into a very firm dough. Cover and rest for 30 mins.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds, then stir in green peas, mashed potatoes, and all spices. Cook for 3–4 minutes. Allow filling to cool completely.
  3. Divide dough into 4 equal balls. Roll each ball into an oval sheet and slice in half to form two semi-circles.
  4. Fold the straight edge of a semi-circle into a cone, sealing the seam with a bit of water.
  5. Stuff the cone with 2 tablespoons of potato filling. Dab water along the top rim and pinch the edges tightly closed.
  6. Heat oil in a deep pan on low-medium heat. Drop samosas gently and deep fry slowly for 12–15 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
  7. Serve hot with mint or tamarind chutney.
