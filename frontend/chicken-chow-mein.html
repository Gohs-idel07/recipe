Chicken Chow Mein Recipe

Serves: 2-3 | Prep & Cooking Time: 25 mins

Ingredients

Main Ingredients:

  • 200g egg noodles
  • 250g chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cabbage, shredded
  • 1/2 cup bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 cup carrots, julienned
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Sauce Mixture:

  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chili sauce
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil egg noodles according to package instructions until al dente. Drain, rinse under cold water, and toss with a drop of oil to avoid sticking.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, dark soy sauce, chili sauce, vinegar, and black pepper. Set aside.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large wok over high heat. Add sliced chicken and stir-fry until cooked (approx. 5 minutes). Remove and set aside.
  4. Add remaining oil to the pan. Toss in cabbage, carrots, and bell peppers. Stir-fry on high heat for 2 minutes to keep them crisp.
  5. Return chicken and boiled noodles to the wok. Pour the sauce mixture evenly over the top.
  6. Toss continuously for 2–3 minutes using tongs or spatulas until sauce evenly coats all noodles.
  7. Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve piping hot.
