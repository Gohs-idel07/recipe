Chicken Fried Rice Recipe

Serves: 3 | Prep & Cooking Time: 20 mins

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice (chilled overnight)
  • 250g boneless chicken, diced small
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup carrots & peas, finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce (optional)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Spring onions for garnish
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Season diced chicken with a pinch of salt and black pepper. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat.
  2. Add chicken and stir-fry for 5–6 minutes until golden brown and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add remaining oil to the pan. Add garlic, diced carrots, and peas. Stir-fry for 2 minutes until vegetables soften.
  4. Push vegetables to one side of the wok, pour beaten eggs into the empty side, and scramble quickly.
  5. Add cooked cold rice and returned chicken to the pan. Break up any rice clumps with your spatula.
  6. Drizzle soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil around the rim of the wok. Stir-fry everything over high heat for 3 minutes.
  7. Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve immediately.
